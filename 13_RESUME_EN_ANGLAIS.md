# 13 - English summary

This dossier maps a set of exploratory contributions around FractalOS/AIONE, TimeWarp, conceptual Batracien/HULK models, the Niffoi information-energy framework, dynamic systems, reproducible simulations, and an AI-assisted self-taught research method.

No physical discovery is claimed. Dimensional, temporal, or spatial distortion is not presented as demonstrated. The strongest current publication candidate is FractalOS/AIONE as a traceability and knowledge-representation architecture for complex interdisciplinary work, because it relies on existing documentation artifacts rather than extraordinary physical assumptions.

The main request is methodological guidance: how to formalize models, identify scientifically relevant parts, detect methodological errors, choose mathematical and computational tools, and progressively transform self-taught exploratory work into a more rigorous and reproducible research process.
